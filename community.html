Candidates

Nominations open after launch. The first round is seeded from community suggestions in our X replies.
How a ballot works: voting sends a 0 ETH transaction on Robinhood Chain to the vault address with the candidate's contract in the data field. Gas costs a fraction of a cent. The tally weighs each wallet by its $MFUEL balance at the round snapshot — one wallet counts once, moving tokens after the snapshot changes nothing. Every vote is public, every count is reproducible from the chain.

Cast an on-chain ballot

Pick a candidate and confirm a 0 ETH transaction carrying MFUEL:VOTE:<token>. That's the whole vote — public, permanent, auditable.

The winner gets a GMGN Manifesto bought by the MANIFEL vault — pinned on their token page and broadcast to every open terminal. Our call history stays public, win or lose.
